      🎴 UNO — How to Play

      1. Match the top card of the discard pile by color or number/symbol, or play a Wild card at any time.
      2. Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two affect the next player. Wild Draw Four also makes the next player draw 4 — you choose the new color for any Wild.
      3. If you can't (or don't want to) play, click Draw Card, then either play the drawn card or click Pass.
      4. Down to 1 card? Click "Call UNO!" — if you forget and someone else notices before your next turn, they can click Catch! and you'll draw 2 penalty cards.
      5. First to empty their hand wins. This game is just for fun — it doesn't affect the tournament leaderboard.

      🃏 Advance Cards (optional house rules)

      Picked per-room in the waiting room before start — any subset, not all-or-nothing.

      1. -2: Plays like a normal card of its color. If you have 5+ cards (including this one), you may also discard up to 2 more cards of the same color in the same turn.
      2. Switch Position: No choice involved — 2 random players at the table (possibly including you) permanently swap seats/turn order.
      3. Action Wild: Choose a color. Repeats whichever of Skip / Reverse / Draw Two was most recently played by anyone this game (no effect if none has been played yet).
      4. Lock: Choose a color. Rolls a die (1-3, weighted toward 1) and randomly skips that many other players' next turn — players holding fewer cards are more likely to be picked.
      5. Switch Position Wild: Choose a color, then pick any 2 players at the table (yourself included) — they permanently swap seats/turn order.
      6. Plus Wild: Choose a color. Every other player immediately draws cards — more if they hold fewer cards (up to 3 for someone at UNO, down to 1 for a big hand). No one is skipped.
